## Case Study 1 — Digital Marketing & Research

CHALLENGE: Launch Maison Hygia to the U.S. Consumer

Maison Hygia is preparing to introduce a premium wellness portfolio under the positioning “Modern Rituals Rooted in Nature.” The initial portfolio spans Botanical Beauty and Ritual Nutrition. The ambition is to create a modern premium wellness brand where nature, ritual, design and technology come together.

## Your Assignment

Assume Maison Hygia gives you a limited initial marketing budget. Develop a launch strategy answering: How would you introduce Maison Hygia to U.S. consumers and acquire the first 1,000 high-quality customers?

## Consumer

- Define 2–3 priority customer personas, their problems, motivations, purchase behavior and why Maison Hygia could appeal to them.

## Competitive Research

- Identify at least five relevant brands and compare positioning, product architecture, pricing, packaging, digital presence, social strategy, strengths and gaps Maison Hygia could exploit.

## Go-to-Market

- Recommend how you would allocate an initial \$25,000 marketing budget across the channels you believe matter most.

- Consider creators, paid social, search, PR, sampling, spas/hotels, partnerships, email/SMS and founder-led content; you do not need to use every channel.

## Content

- Create one launch campaign concept, three social posts, one short-form video/reel concept, one creator collaboration concept and one landing-page concept.

## Measurement & Research

- Identify 5–7 metrics you would monitor during the first 90 days.

- Find three realistic U.S. partners, creators, retailers, spas, hotels, agencies or other organizations that could help Maison Hygia. Explain why and how you would approach them.

## Deliverable

Maximum 8 slides/pages. Include sources and assumptions.


## Case Study 2 — Technology & Marketplace

CHALLENGE: Build the Digital Commerce Foundation

Maison Hygia wants to build a premium digital commerce platform that can eventually support Beauty, Nutrition, Wellness, personalized recommendations, subscriptions, bundles, customer accounts, loyalty, reviews, inventory, promotions, U.S. payments and AI-powered experiences.

## Your Assignment

Design the technical architecture for MaisonHygia.com and build one small working component.

## Architecture

- Choose and justify frontend, backend, database, API architecture, authentication, catalog, cart, checkout/payment integration, search, hosting/cloud, CDN, image storage, analytics, security and CI/CD.

## Data Model & APIs

- Design the data model for Product → Variant → Inventory → Cart → Order → Customer.

- Design REST or GraphQL interfaces for retrieving products, searching products, adding to cart, creating an order and retrieving customer orders.

## Coding Component

- Build one small working component such as a Product API, search service, shopping cart, product listing interface, recommendation component or checkout prototype.

- Provide code/repository, README and run instructions.

## Scale Scenario

- Traffic suddenly increases from 500 visitors/day to 100,000 visitors/day after influencer exposure. Explain what breaks first and how you would scale or redesign it.

## Deliverable

Maximum 8 slides/pages plus code repository or source files. Clean engineering decisions matter more than unnecessary complexity.


## Case Study 3 — AI & Innovation

CHALLENGE: Reinvent Product Discovery with AI

Maison Hygia believes commerce is moving from search-and-browse toward need-driven, AI-assisted discovery. Imagine a customer says: “My skin feels dry lately, I’m not sleeping particularly well, and I want to create a better evening routine without making it complicated.”

## Your Assignment

Design an AI Ritual Concierge that understands the consumer, retrieves relevant product knowledge, recommends an appropriate ritual and explains its reasoning without making inappropriate medical claims.

## Experience Design

- Show how the system understands intent, asks follow-up questions, retrieves product information, recommends a ritual, explains selections and learns from later interactions.

## AI Architecture

- Design an architecture using the technologies you believe are appropriate: LLMs, RAG, vector databases, recommendation systems, customer profiles, knowledge graphs, agents, memory, guardrails and analytics.

- Explain why you chose the architecture rather than simply listing technologies.

## Prototype

- Create a small prototype demonstrating at least one important component: conversational assistant, recommendation engine, RAG prototype, agent workflow or scoring model. Synthetic data is acceptable.

## Trust & Optimization

- Assume the AI starts recommending the most expensive products because they generate higher margins. Explain why this is problematic and design an objective that balances consumer relevance, trust and business outcomes.

## Deliverable

Maximum 8 slides/pages plus prototype/code where applicable.


## Case Study 4 — Product Innovation

## CHALLENGE: Invent the Next Maison Hygia Ritual

Maison Hygia spans botanical beauty and ritual nutrition under the positioning “Modern Rituals Rooted in Nature.” We want products that solve recognizable consumer problems while creating an elevated ritual experience.

## Your Assignment

Identify one unmet or underserved consumer need and invent a new Maison Hygia product or ritual around it. It may belong to Beauty, Hair, Bath & Body, Nutrition, Wellness, Recovery, or a new category.

## Consumer Problem

- Who experiences the problem, how do they solve it today, and why are current solutions inadequate?

## Market Research

- Identify at least three existing products or competitors addressing the need and explain what Maison Hygia could do differently.

## Product Concept

- Define product name, positioning, format, hero ingredients, supporting ingredients, usage ritual, size, packaging and target retail price.

## Formulation Thinking

- Create a conceptual formula and distinguish between base, functional ingredients, botanical actives, sensory components and preservation/stability considerations.

## Commercial & Packaging Thinking

- Estimate target COGS, wholesale price, retail price and gross margin using reasonable assumptions.

- Create one packaging concept/mockup consistent with Maison Hygia while introducing something distinctive.

## Innovation Question

- Answer: Why should this product exist? Do not answer only that the market is growing; explain what genuinely makes it useful, different or desirable.

## Deliverable

Maximum 8 slides/pages plus one visual concept.
